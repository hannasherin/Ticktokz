ABOUT OUR WATCH SERVICE

Time is not just a clock, but it is the most precious asset in your life. When you wear a luxury watch — whether it’s from Tissot, Rado, Longines, Omega, or any other Swiss brand — it’s not just a time-telling device, but a symbol of your personality and success. Therefore, it must be cared for with the same expertise and precision.

  • Prevents Wear & Tear — Lubrication and inspection reduce friction between parts, extending the lifespan of delicate mechanisms.
  • Professional Cleaning — Removes dirt, dust, and residue that can damage both the interior mechanism and exterior finish.
  • Longevity Assurance — Regular care can keep a watch running perfectly for decades, even generations.
  • Aesthetic Appeal — Polishing and maintenance preserve the shine and elegance of your watch, keeping it looking new.
  • Early Problem Detection — Experts can identify issues like loose parts, rust, or battery leakage before they become costly repairs.

WHY IS REGULAR WATCH SERVICING IMPORTANT?

Often customers wonder why the clock would need service if it is running right. But the reality is that there are many delicate parts within the luxury mechanical and quartz watch. Over time, they are affected by dust, moisture, and wear. If there is no timely service, this not only affects the accuracy of the watch, but can also damage its entire mechanism.

At TickToks Watch Company, we closely examine every watch. Our aim is to ensure that your watch not only runs perfect, but also keeps its look, finish, and true beauty for a long time.
